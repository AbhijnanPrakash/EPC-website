Overview

Data Center Infrastructure

A dependable data center is more than a UPS. Eternal Power builds out the physical layer around your IT load: racks that keep equipment organized, rack-based cooling that scales with the load, power distribution units and static switches that keep single-corded equipment on redundant power, precision cooling for the room, and DCIM software that turns the whole installation into something you can plan and operate proactively.

Scope Racks, cooling, power distribution, monitoring
Cooling Rack-based and precision room cooling
Power path PDUs, static transfer switches, redundant feeds

FAQ

Frequently asked

Can you upgrade an existing server room in phases?

Yes. Rack-based cooling and modular power distribution are designed for phased upgrades, so you improve reliability without a disruptive rebuild.

Do you maintain the cooling as well as the power?

Yes. AMC coverage can span UPS, cooling, distribution and the monitoring layer, so one team is accountable for the room.
