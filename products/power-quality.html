Overview

Power Monitoring & Power Quality

Most power quality disturbances, around 80 percent, are generated by equipment inside your own facility: drives, welders, large motors, UPS systems and IT loads. They show up as unscheduled downtime, equipment damage and utility penalties. Eternal Power supplies metering and monitoring that make your electrical system visible, and correction equipment that fixes what the data reveals.

Instrumentation Energy meters, multifunction meters, EMS software
Correction Power factor correction and harmonic filtering equipment
Outcomes Penalty avoidance, capacity recovery, verified savings

FAQ

Frequently asked

What does a power quality problem look like day to day?

Nuisance tripping, unexplained equipment failures, overheating cables or transformers, flickering, and penalties on your utility bill are the usual symptoms. An audit identifies the underlying cause.

Is power factor correction really worth the investment?

In facilities paying PF or demand penalties, correction equipment typically pays for itself quickly, it removes penalties and recovers usable capacity from transformers and cables.
