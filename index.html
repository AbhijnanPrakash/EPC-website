How we work

Our collaborative process

Six stages from first site visit to long-term support. Select a stage to see what happens in it.

Site study

An engineer visits the facility, measures the actual connected load, reviews the incoming supply and records the failure modes that matter to you — process equipment, medical devices, servers or lifts. Nothing is sized from a phone call.

Design & sizing

We produce the single-line diagram, UPS capacity and battery bank sizing against your target runtime, plus the power quality corrections the site needs. Redundancy is decided deliberately, not defaulted to.

Proposal

A specific recommendation with make, model, configuration, delivery timeline and commercial terms. Where there is a cheaper option that still meets the brief, it is on the same page as the preferred one.

Installation

Supply, mechanical installation, cabling and integration with existing distribution, scheduled around your operations. Site relocation and recommissioning of existing equipment are handled the same way.

Commissioning

Load testing, calibration, battery bank verification and handover documentation, followed by operator training so your facility team knows what the alarms mean before they see one.

Support & AMC

Preventive maintenance on a written schedule, emergency breakdown cover round the clock, and optional 24×7 remote monitoring so degradation is caught from the command center before it becomes an outage.

Expert insights

Questions engineers actually ask

The technical and commercial questions that come up most often before a project starts.

What UPS capacity range does Eternal Power supply?

Online UPS from 1 kVA standalone units through to 4000 kVA parallel-redundant systems, covering a 350 VA to 4.8 MW system range. Sizing is driven by a measured load study rather than a catalogue guess.

Do you service UPS systems you did not sell?

Yes. Our comprehensive and non-comprehensive annual maintenance contracts cover UPS of any make, alongside BMS, fire alarm panels and electrical distribution equipment.

How quickly can you respond to a breakdown?

AMC customers get round-the-clock emergency breakdown support. Response times are written into the contract rather than left to a call centre script, and sites under remote monitoring are often diagnosed before the customer calls.

Which battery types and brands do you work with?

SMF, VRLA and Li-Ion batteries from 7 AH to 400 AH, including Panasonic, Exide, Quanta and EnerRocket. Bank health is verified with HIOKI internal-resistance testing before any replacement is recommended.

Do you work outside Bangalore?

Bangalore and the wider Karnataka region are our core service area. Multi-site customers are supported from our command centre over GPRS/IP, so monitoring and analytics extend beyond the sites our engineers reach on the same day.
