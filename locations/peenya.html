FAQs — Peenya

What UPS size do Peenya factories usually need?
It depends on measured control and IT load, not floor area. We do a load study on site before recommending kVA and battery AH.
Do you service older industrial UPS brands?
Yes. AMC and repair support are available for any make.
Can you replace batteries without a full UPS change?
Yes, after IR testing and compatibility checks with the existing charger and UPS.
