How this service runs

Site readiness check

Pre-installation audit to ensure foundation and space meet technical standards.

Installation & termination

Physical placement of equipment and precision electrical wiring.

Earthing & safety

Grounding verification and safety interlock validation.

Functional test

Initial power-up and sequence testing of control panels.

Commissioning test

Full-load testing to verify performance parameters and reliability.

Handover

Final certification delivery and operator briefing.
