Service

UPS & Battery Rental / Buyback

Rental UPS and battery banks for emergencies and short-term needs, plus buyback of retired equipment.

SERVESEvents, Construction & fit-outs, IT & offices, Industrial

Overview

Some power problems are temporary: an event, a fit-out, a failed unit awaiting replacement. Rental UPS and battery banks solve the emergency without capital spend. And when equipment reaches end of life, our buyback service takes old UPS systems, batteries, IT assets and workstations off your hands with a documented process.

What's included

  • Rental UPS systems sized to the temporary load
  • Rental battery banks for extended autonomy
  • Delivery, installation and pickup
  • Buyback of UPS systems, batteries, IT assets and workstations

Benefits

Emergency cover

A rental unit keeps you running while a failed system is repaired or replaced.

No capital spend

Short-term needs met without buying equipment you will not need next quarter.

Clean exits

Buyback removes retired equipment with documentation, not scrap-dealer ambiguity.
